Vice Frame turns ordinary photos into a GTA-poster-style cover, right in your browser and completely offline. Lay pictures out as a big hero panel with a stacked pair beside it, a clean 2×2 grid, three vertical strips, or one full-bleed shot. Click a panel to select it, drag to reframe it, scroll or use the slider to zoom, and tune the white, black or gold gutters between panels.

Over the collage sits a sunset grade — teal at the top melting into hot pink at the bottom, with a soft vignette — in three flavours: Sunset, Neon and Gold. Add a small letterspaced kicker and a heavy italic caption with a thick outline, place the words at the top or bottom, then export a PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Five built-in sample photos get you a finished cover in seconds; your own uploads never leave the machine. All artwork and lettering are original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
